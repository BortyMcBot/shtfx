## Summary

This PR adds the first set of **generic, reusable** threat research guides plus a fully fictional example profile for SHTFx users.

## Included

- Wildfire preparedness guide
- Earthquake preparedness guide
- Grid failure preparedness guide
- Water supply disruption preparedness guide
- Civil unrest preparedness guide
- Fictional example profile under `profiles/example-profile.json`

## Policy note

All content in this PR is intentionally **generic and location-agnostic** per the public/private separation policy.

- No real family data
- No household-specific information
- No place-specific details
- No location-specific threat research

Location-specific findings, household profile data, intake logs, and rendered handbook outputs remain in the private repo.
